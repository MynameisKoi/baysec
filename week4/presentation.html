DEBRIEF & WALKTHROUGH

0x01 Solution: Client-Side Exposure

Analyzing how client-side data leaks compromise authentication systems.

STEP 1: INSPECT THE DOM
  • Press F12 or Right-Click ➔ Inspect Element
  • Check comments embedded directly inside the <body>:
<!-- The flag is here:
c2ZidXt3M2xjMG0zX3QwX2I0eXMzY18yMDI2fQ== -->
Notice the trailing == padding — the trademark tell of Base64 encoding.
STEP 2: DECODE BASE64

METHOD A: TERMINAL PIPELINE

echo "c2ZidXt3M2xjMG0zX3QwX2I0eXMzY18yMDI2fQ==" | base64 -d

METHOD B: CYBERCHEF

Paste into CyberChef ➔ Add recipe "From Base64"

FLAG RECOVERED:
sfbu{w3lc0m3_t0_b4ys3c_2026}
⚠️ KEY TAKEAWAY: Never store secrets, comments, or sensitive auth logic inside client-rendered markup. Base64 is encoding, not encryption.
